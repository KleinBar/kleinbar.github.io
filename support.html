How do I play?

Every board hides exactly one cat that appears three times. Tap all three as fast as you can. Faster finds earn more time.

How do I remove the ads?

On the Home screen tap the gear icon, then Remove ads. It is a one-time purchase: the ads between games are gone for good. Reward videos (for example "Continue" after a run) stay, and only play when you choose them.

I bought Remove ads but I see ads again (new phone or reinstall)

Tap the gear icon on the Home screen, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. On iPhone and iPad, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play refunds.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: gear icon → Privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Will I lose my cats if I delete the game?

Your progress (album, scores, settings) is stored only on your device, so deleting the game deletes it. A purchase of Remove ads can always be restored.

Sound, vibration, shake and flash

Gear icon on the Home screen: turn music, sound and vibration on or off, and turn on Reduce shake & flash for calmer effects.
