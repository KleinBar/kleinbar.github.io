Triple Paws Privacy Policy

Triple Paws ("the game") is developed by AntelopApp. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device. Your scores, discovered cats, progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not have access to them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob). The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate approximate location) and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad, the advertising ID is used only if you choose Allow when Apple asks whether the game may track you; you can change this anytime in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase). We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which modes are played and how long sessions last) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. This data is associated with a random app-instance identifier, not your name or contact details. Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases (Google Play / App Store). The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details; the store only tells the game that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore it on a new device.

Remote settings (Google Firebase Remote Config). The game downloads a few settings (for example, how often ads may appear) so we can adjust them without an update. This uses a random Firebase installation identifier, not your name or contact details.

Children. The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing. We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is processed by Google as our service provider under its own privacy policy.

Your rights. You may request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, or ask any privacy question, by writing to kleinbarsi@gmail.com.

Changes. We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change.
